Purpose

FOUNDRY Lab is dedicated to advancing intelligent AI systems that can perceive, understand, reason, and act by integrating information across multiple modalities, including vision, language, audio, and autonomous agents. The lab conducts cutting-edge research in machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, multimodal AI, large language models, and agentic systems, fostering innovation through interdisciplinary collaboration and real-world applications.
